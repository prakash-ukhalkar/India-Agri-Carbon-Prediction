# India Agri-Carbon Prediction Datasets

The following table catalogs the raw datasets available in the `data/raw/` directory, which are used for modeling and prediction of agricultural carbon metrics.

| Sr. No. | Dataset Title | Period | Brief Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FAOSTAT Emissions from Energy Use (All) | Varies | Total emissions from energy use across agricultural operations. |
| 2 | FAOSTAT Agricultural Energy Use Emissions | Varies | Emissions specifically from energy consumption in agriculture. |
| 3 | FAOSTAT Crop Emissions | Varies | Emissions produced directly from crop cultivation and residues. |
| 4 | Agricultural Electricity Consumption | 1995-96 to 2024-25 | Annual electricity usage in kWh for the Indian agricultural sector. |
| 5 | Irrigation Water Usage Proxy | 1996-2025 | Proxy data for irrigation water consumption from WDI. |
| 6 | Agricultural Energy Usage Proxy | 1995-96 to 2024-25 | Proxy metrics for renewable and non-renewable energy use. |
| 7 | Land-level Electricity Allocation | 1995-96 to 2024-25 | Agricultural electricity consumption at the land level. |
| 8 | Irrigated Land Proxy | 1996-2025 | World Bank proxy data estimating irrigated land area. |
| 9 | Rainfed Land Proxy | 1996-2025 | World Bank proxy data estimating rainfed agricultural land. |
| 10 | FAOSTAT Land Use | Varies | General agricultural land use statistics from FAOSTAT. |
| 11 | Geographic Location Data | 1996-2025 | Latitude, longitude, and altitude statistics for Indian agricultural regions. |
| 12 | Soil Texture Quantitative Data | 1996-2025 | Quantitative metrics describing soil texture across farming regions. |
| 13 | Soil Macro Nutrients & pH | 2023-24 | Soil Health Dashboard data covering macronutrients and pH. |
| 14 | Soil Macro Nutrients & pH | 2024-25 | Soil macro nutrient and pH metrics. |
| 15 | Soil Macro Nutrients & pH | 2025-26 | Projected/latest soil macro nutrient and pH metrics. |
| 16 | Soil Micro Nutrients & pH | 2023-24 | Soil Health Dashboard data covering micronutrients and pH. |
| 17 | Soil Micro Nutrients & pH | 2024-25 | Soil Health Dashboard data covering micronutrients and pH. |
| 18 | Soil Micro Nutrients & pH | 2025-26 | Projected/latest soil micro nutrient and pH metrics. |
| 19 | India Humidity Data (Kaggle) | Varies | Dataset with humidity metrics for Indian regions. |
| 20 | India Rainfall Data (IMD) | Varies | Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) rainfall statistics. |
| 21 | India Temperature Data (IMD) | Varies | IMD temperature observations for India. |
| 22 | India Crop Yields (Kaggle) | 1997-2020 | Historical crop yields in India. |
| 23 | Crop Yield/Production Report | Varies | General cross-tabulated report of crop production over time. |
| 24 | Crop Yield/Production Report | 1997-2000 | Early crop yield and production statistics. |
| 25 | Crop Yield/Production Report | 2000-2006 | Mid-term crop yield and production statistics. |
| 26 | Crop Yield/Production Report | 2007-2011 | More recent historical crop yield and production data. |
| 27 | Crop Yield/Production Report | 2012-2022 | Contemporary crop yield and production data. |
| 28 | FAOSTAT Fertilizer Use | Varies | Volumes and types of fertilizers used in agriculture. |
| 29 | Fertilizer Consumption History | 1951-2024 | Long-term historical report on fertilizer consumption in India. |
| 30 | FAOSTAT Livestock Manure | Varies | Application and availability of livestock manure. |
| 31 | Agricultural Machinery Hours | 1996-2025 | Hours logged for agricultural machinery operations. |
| 32 | FAOSTAT Pesticide Use | Varies | Quantities of pesticides applied in agriculture. |
